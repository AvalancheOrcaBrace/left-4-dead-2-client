## 🧩 About

**Left 4 Dead 2 Client** is a comprehensive overlay and menu for Left 4 Dead 2 featuring ESP for special infected, aim assist, item and weapon radar, config presets, and campaign utilities.

Based on popular community tools like **SourceMod plugins**, **L4D2 overlay menus**, and **QOL clients**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Features
- ESP — highlight special infected, common infected, and teammates through walls
- Aim Assist — adjustable smoothing and FOV for co-op play
- Radar — mini-map with loot, weapons, and health items
- No Recoil — stabilize weapon spread during panic events
- Bunny Hop — toggle auto-jump for movement practice

### 🎨 HUD & Overlay
- Custom HUD — health, ammo, and timer widgets
- Overlay Menu — toggle with `Insert` or `Tab`
- Config Presets — save and load per-campaign setups
- Stream-Proof Mode — hide overlay from capture software

### 🏋️ Campaign & Co-op Tools
- Item Tracker — locate pills, medkits, and throwables
- Tank & Witch Alerts — on-screen warnings
- Server Browser Helper — quick-join preferred lobbies
- Config Sync — share presets with your co-op team
